# Financing evidence and scenario choices

Reviewed October 2, 2026 UTC. Only disclosures published by August 3, 2026 inform this analysis. Later realized balances and prices are excluded. Numerical observations below are source-linked anchors, not estimates of future demand.

## Observations

| Observation | Value | Locator |
|---|---:|---|
| Q2 2026 common ATM net proceeds | $2.943342bn | Q2 10-Q, Note 12 |
| Q2 2026 STRC ATM net proceeds | $5.455658bn | Q2 10-Q, Note 12 |
| June 30 available common program capacity | $24.2575bn | Q2 10-Q, Capital Markets Activity |
| June 30 available STRC program capacity | $17.5108bn | Same table |

These observations anchor scale; program capacity does not establish investor demand. Net proceeds cannot identify issue fees without matching gross proceeds. The $6bn model budgets are deliberately separate scenario constraints, not observed remaining authority. The model's $400m growth offer per channel on 13 dates totals $5.2bn gross per channel across 15 months. Lower average issuance than a historical quarter does not make future access assured. [S1]

The July 6 filing reports no ATM sales in June 29–July 5. It motivates retaining zero-access cases without treating one week as a forecast of sustained closure. [S2]

## Terms and their limited use

The omnibus agreement defines sales commissions up to 2%, or otherwise agreed, and separately assigns expenses. Its settlement definition is the next business day. Therefore 0.25%, 1%, and 2% all-in model frictions are sensitivities, not measured fees or rigorous bounds on all-in costs. Same-event cash in the frozen engine is an aggregation approximation. [S3]

The STRC annex states an intended, discretionary $99–$101 issuance range. The model tests the central $100 execution case, without inferring demand from that price. [S4] The June framework supplies the 12-month reserve policy, restricted reserve purposes, $1.25bn reserve-building monetization authority, and announced 12% forward STRC rate. It does not establish the model's $1.1bn general-use buffer or $2bn additional general-use sale grant. [S5]

## Unestimated quantities

| Quantity | Analysis choice | Evidentiary status |
|---|---|---|
| Investor capacity | 0–100% of the v0.4 reference ceilings | Stress range; not a fitted demand curve |
| Common residual-backing multiple | Initial 1.25; terminal 0.75–1.75 | Assumed valuation paths; not Strategy's enterprise mNAV or an estimated historical range |
| Cash retained from purchases | $0–$1.2bn; reference $1.1bn | Allocation alternatives motivated by dated uses, not an optimum |
| Future STRC rate | 10%, 12%, 14% from January 2027 | Symmetric cost sensitivities around the announced forward rate; not predicted adjustments |
| Common growth gate | Net issue premium ≥1 | Conditional accretion rule, not a disclosed company threshold |
| Additional general-purpose sale authority | $0 or $2bn | Explicit alternative authorization assumptions |

The evidence supports these distinctions and useful stress comparisons. It does not select a unique future financing path, common premium, or cash policy. None of the map axes should be called empirically calibrated probabilities or defensible outer bounds.
